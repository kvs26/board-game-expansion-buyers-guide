## Gotchas
- Amazon `/dp/` pages often return no `<title>` after a few requests; retries with gaps were needed. ASIN B07T2FRHD8 (guessed as New Age) and B077Y6Z9KY (guessed as The Void) never returned a title, so they were not used.
- A third-party site labelled B072BG9Z9V as The Descent; the Amazon title shows it is Aeon's End 2e.
- Publisher "Buy from Amazon" links for several small boxes 404 (guessed slugs), so they were not trusted.
- BGG pages are behind a Cloudflare challenge for curl; they are cited as fact sources but were read through a fetch tool.

## Review-copy and affiliate disclosures
- No review copies are involved in the sources used.
- Puzzlewick's guide is an affiliate site (tag puzzlewick-20) and AI-assisted; it is used only to corroborate The Descent ranking and is flagged in that row's notes.

## Skipped
- New Age and Outcasts: the most recommended standalone big boxes in the threads, but no verifiable Amazon listing or publisher page was found in time. This is a real gap in the guide.
- Legacy and Legacy of Gravehold (separate standalone campaign products), Southern Village, The Caverns and The Abyss (Gamefound stretch boxes), Origins and Evolution, Beyond the Breach (no MSRP found), accessory packs and storage, crowdfunding promos, digital versions.
