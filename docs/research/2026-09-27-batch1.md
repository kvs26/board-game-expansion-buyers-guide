## Edition gotchas
- CATAN is on the **6th edition** (2025 boxes). The shop says expansions "require CATAN Base Game 6th edition". Whether 5th-edition parts mix with 6th isn't verified.
- CATAN 5-6 extensions for expansions need **four boxes**: base, base 5-6, the expansion, and its 5-6 box.
- Traders & Barbarians is the core CATAN box that adds 2-player rules.
- TTR Map Collection needs **either** TTR (USA) or TTR Europe for trains and cards. USA 1910 works **only** with the original USA game. France/Old West adds a 6th train color. The UK map is 2–4 players, while its Pennsylvania map is 2–5.
- Carcassonne current Z-Man edition (v3): the base box includes The River and The Abbot. Z-Man lists Expansions 1–6 and 8–10. **Expansion 7 isn't on the site.** Under the Big Top shows "Currently unavailable".
- Inns & Cathedrals is the expansion that adds the 6th player.
