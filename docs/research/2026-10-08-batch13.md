# Research batch 13 — 2026-10-08

Game: Power Grid (base row is Power Grid: Recharged, Rio Grande Games, $49.95). 11 add-ons covered.

## Sources and gotchas
- Facts: Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Power_Grid_(board_game)) for original release years; BGG pages for Middle East/South Africa (2019) and Europe/North America (2021).
- Prices, player counts and MSRP: Rio Grande Games product pages. riograndegames.com returns a Cloudflare 403 to curl, fetch and the browser, so I read the pages through the Wayback Machine (`web.archive.org/web/2026/<url>`). Rio lists every add-on as 2-6 players, 90-120 minutes; I used 120.
- Price is MSRP from Rio Grande. GameNerdz showed $12.57 for Europe/North America; I treated that as a retailer discount and did not use it.
- Amazon: ASINs came from Bing results decoded in the browser. All 12 ASINs used (base + 11) were checked with curl and the titles match.
- Rejected ASINs: B087QPTYCG (Australia/India) returns the title "Rio Grande Games RIO576 Accessories, not Applicable". B095J644DY (France/Italy Recharged) returned no title. Neither is used.
- Benelux/Central Europe, Quebec/Baden-Württemberg, Brazil and Russia/Japan Amazon titles may be older printings; the Rio pages show the Recharged edition as the current one.
- Europe/North America and Middle East/South Africa Rio pages were out of print or out of stock when archived.
- The first terminal commands in the session got interrupted by other agents; later checks used async runs written to /tmp files.
