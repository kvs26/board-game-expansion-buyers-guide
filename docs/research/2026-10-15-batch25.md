## Edition and compatibility caveats
- Isle of Cats: Kickstarter Pack 2 needs Kittens + Beasts. Both Kickstarter packs are now sold by the publisher directly (the US store had Pack 2 out of stock on 2026-10-15). Only Late Arrivals supports 5-6 players.
- Orléans: the 8th printing (2023) already includes 5th-player material, so the 5-Player Expansion only matters for older printings. Deluxe Edition, Big Box and Orléans Stories are out of scope.
- Obsession: the 2nd edition is the one in the Kayenta shop; Upstairs, Downstairs enables 5 players (6 also needs the Wessex family board). Characters does not need Upstairs, Downstairs (designer reply in thread 3189532).
- Azul: Crystal Mosaic needs the original Azul; one new board scores differently, so do not mix old and new boards.
- Mysterium: an original edition (2-7 players) and a 2025 Refresh (3-7 players). BGG posters disagree on whether expansion cards mix across editions. The CSV uses the Libellud/Refresh card counts and the Refresh Amazon listing (B0FSDT2333); the original Hidden Signs listing (B01D6MTQ4O) was verified but not used.
- Marvel United: Blue Team, Gold Team, First Class, Days of Future Past, Fantastic Four and Deadpool are expansions, not standalone boxes, even though the assignment grouped them with standalone boxes (BGG subtype, CMON). All 23 rows are `base_game_required=conditional` with one rule: a Marvel United core box is needed (the original, or another core box such as X-Men). The core-box requirement is verified only for Maximum Carnage (r/MarvelUnited); for the others it is inferred from BGG links and the line.
- Marvel United Kickstarter exclusives (no retail page found): Return of the Sinister Six, The Infinity Gauntlet, First Class, Days of Future Past, Fantastic Four, Phoenix Five, X-Force, Horsemen of Apocalypse, The Coming of Galactus. Retail status of Secret Invasion, War of Kings, Annihilation and Age of Apocalypse is not verified.
- Cthulhu: Season 3 is sold as Fear of the Unknown (standalone box, also mixes with Season 1). Season 4 needs a core box (Season 1, Season 3 or Season 5). R'lyeh Rising has a 2019 edition (BGG 260936) and a 2024 edition (BGG 425689) with no monster cards. Unspeakable and Unknowable Boxes are Kickstarter bundles with retail release TBD on the Jan 2026 Asmodee list. Season 2 and Black Goat are described as out of print by BGG and Reddit posters (not checked at a retailer).
- Clank! Catacombs is a standalone game separate from Clank! and also accepts cards from earlier Clank! expansions. Clank!: Adventuring Party was not added.
- Arcs: the retail base box is named "Arcs: Conflict & Collapse in The Reach". The first printing of The Blighted Reach has errata (a $2 Errata Pack sold separately). The Blighted Reach does not need Leaders & Lore.

## Unverified fields and gaps
- No Amazon link was found for The Isle of Cats, Cthulhu: Death May Die, Arcs, SETI, Roll Player or Clank! Catacombs; Amazon search is blocked and Bing did not surface `/dp` pages. Those rows use publisher shop pages.
- Rows whose only buy link is a BGG reference row (no publisher or Amazon page found): Orléans 5-Player Expansion; Marvel United Return of the Sinister Six, Days of Future Past, Fantastic Four, Infinity Gauntlet, First Class, World War Hulk, Secret Invasion, Age of Apocalypse, Horsemen of Apocalypse, War of Kings, Annihilation, X-Force, Phoenix Five; Cthulhu Season 4, Black Goat of the Woods, Unspeakable Box, Unknowable Box, Animal Allies, R'lyeh Rising. The Asmodee store page for Season 4 redirected to the home page.
- Prices are publisher-store prices seen on 2026-10-15 unless the `price_kind` says MSRP; they are not MSRP statements. The Dire Wolf store currency (Clank! Catacombs) is assumed to be USD. The Plague at $12.00 (Capstone) may be a sale price. The Marvel United Blue and Gold Team Asmodee price ($34.99) is $5 above CMON's MSRP. No price was found for the five Marvel United Kickstarter exclusives or for R'lyeh Rising.
- Player counts for expansions mostly use BGG values. Where BGG's maximum needs another expansion (Isle of Cats 5-6 players, Roll Player Monsters & Minions fifth player) the entered maximum follows the base requirement.
- `what_it_fixes` is blank for most rows (no documented fix on publisher pages or BGG text). Space Agencies has only the Quick Start cards, which replace the slow opening round.
- `complexity_change` and `group_style` are blank where no source supports them (Isle of Cats Kickstarter Pack 2 and Treasure Pack, and the unranked rows).
- Release years are BGG years (often the Kickstarter year). The Treasure Pack's 2023 comes from the shop publish date.
- Story-card counts for Mysterium: Secrets & Lies conflict across sources (18 / 16 / 32), so no count is stated. Crystal Mosaic's board count is not stated for the same reason.
- BGG poster usernames are not returned by the API, so opinions are attributed to "BGG posters".
- Whether The Plague can be stacked with Trade & Intrigue is disputed in BGG thread 3205339; no official statement found.
