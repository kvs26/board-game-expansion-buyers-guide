## Sources and gotchas

- Prices: store.asmodee.com `products.json` gives $47.99 for in-print boxes (NeXt Evolution, Age of Apocalypse, Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D., Civil War, Fear No Evil) and $18.99 for the Venom hero pack. Core Set $69.99.
- Older boxes (Rise of the Red Skull, Galaxy's Most Wanted, Mad Titan's Shadow, Sinister Motives, Mutant Genesis) are no longer on the Asmodee store (404); $44.99 is the prior Asmodee list price read from Wayback, recorded as `msrp`.
- Box descriptions (heroes, scenario counts, campaign numbering) come from the Asmodee store product text.
- Amazon: dp pages returned a captcha during this run and other agents were running concurrently. ASIN search results were misaligned (e.g. B0F94K3N71 is a "Sleeve Kings" sleeve bundle, rejected). All Amazon ASIN/URL fields are intentionally blank, following the batch15 precedent.
