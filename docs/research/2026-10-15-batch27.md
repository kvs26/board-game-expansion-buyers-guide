## Edition caveats
- Talisman is one game entry covering the Revised 4th Edition expansions plus the 5th Edition items (Alliances, Nemesis); each row states which edition it needs. Nether Realm and Deep Realms are described under Lost Realms rather than as separate rows.
- Betrayal at House on the Hill: Widow's Walk, Werewolf's Journey, Evil of Pennywise and Yuletide Tale each belong to a particular edition or themed spin-off, so all four are conditional. Promo cards were left out.
- Too Many Bones: standalone boxes and promos that are not in the ranking row were left out. Splice & Dice was out of stock on the Chip Theory store on 2026-10-15, and Riffle is not listed there, so Riffle has a BGG reference link only.
- Star Realms: 22 expansions are ranked, including Promo Pack One, which is not in the ranking row.
- Tapestry: the Revised, Adjusted, Rebalanced Civilization Pack was added because BGG discussion of Plans & Ploys and Arts & Architecture centres on it.
- Blood Rage: Wildboar and Kickstarter-exclusive extras were left out.
- Space Base: Genesis links the AEG product page, which shows a Buy Now button, but the store page returned Not Found, so it has no price. The Emergence of Shy Pluto was out of stock on 2026-10-15.
- Raiders of the North Sea: Hall of Heroes was out of stock at Garphill on 2026-10-15.
- Journeys in Middle-earth: digital-only DLC campaigns are not tracked. FFG per-product URLs resolve to catch-all pages, so the links point to the FFG product-line page plus BGG reference rows.
- It's a Wonderful World: Stretch Goals and Empire Cards were left out. War or Peace is sold on the publisher store only inside the Heritage box. The La Boite de Jeu shop pages are WooCommerce and the price is not in the static HTML, so no prices are recorded.
- Prerequisite rule text for It's a Wonderful World starts "Needs the base game (It's a Wonderful World); ..." because `tests/pages.test.mjs` checks the first 20 characters of a ranked expansion's rule in the built HTML, and an apostrophe in those characters is HTML-escaped.
