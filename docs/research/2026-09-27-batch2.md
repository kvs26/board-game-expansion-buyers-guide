## Dominion (15 rows)
- Sources: Wikipedia; Dominion Strategy wiki "Sets" (release dates and editions) and "Base Cards"; IGN Dominion Buying Guide (updated June 2024), which gave the MSRPs, the player counts for Intrigue and Menagerie, and the top picks.
- Verdicts: Intrigue as first pick (Wikipedia, citing TechRaptor and Rio Grande). Seaside, Plunder, Nocturne and Menagerie come from IGN's top picks only, so each rests on one reviewer and is weak. The other 10 rows have no verdict.
- Not verified: player counts for most sets. IGN says 2–6 for Seaside, Plunder and Nocturne, but that probably depends on owning extra base cards, so I left them blank. No prices for sets outside IGN's picks.
- **Edition gotchas**:
  - Intrigue **1st edition (2009) was standalone** with its own basic cards. The **2nd edition (2016) is not**: it needs the base game or the separate **Base Cards** set.
  - Seaside, Prosperity and Hinterlands got 2nd editions in 2022. Cornucopia & Guilds got a combined 2nd edition in 2024, and the two are no longer sold separately. Some cards were swapped out, and Update Packs exist for owners of 1st editions.
  - Every expansion needs basic Treasure and Victory cards from the base game or the Base Cards set.
  - Arcana is expected in 2026 and was left out.

## Race for the Galaxy (5 rows)
- Sources: Wikipedia; BGG thread 3281425, "Which expansions are must have"; BGG page for Alien Artifacts (2–5 players, 2013, not compatible with arc 1).
- Consensus: get The Gathering Storm first. Alien Artifacts and Xeno Invasion are well liked single-box options. The rest of arc 1 is for 6-player games or players who want more fighting.
- **Gotcha**: the three arcs can't be mixed. Rebel vs Imperium is meant to be played with The Gathering Storm, and The Brink of War completes arc 1. BGG users say a **first-arc big box** exists, and Wei-Hwa Huang mentions **Xeno Counterstrike**. I did not check either, so neither is in the CSV.
- Not verified: release years for arc 1, the minimum players for RvI, BoW and XI, and all prices.

## 7 Wonders (4 rows)
- Sources: rprod.com pages for the game, Leaders, Cities, Armada and Edifice (all say 3–7 players); rprod FAQ; Wikipedia; everythingisagame.com expansion guide; BGG 2E expansion poll thread.
- **Edition gotcha**: the rprod FAQ says **do not mix 1st and 2nd edition** base games and expansions. You can tell the 2nd edition by the metallic title on the box. everythingisagame claims most expansions work with both editions, which contradicts the FAQ. Trust the FAQ. Edifice is the only exception: the publisher says it works with 1st edition too.
- Babel, Wonder Pack and the Anniversary packs are 1st edition only. I skipped them, along with the promos.
- Not verified: 2nd-edition release years for Leaders, Cities and Armada, and all prices. Expansion ASINs appear only in third-party affiliate links: Leaders B08FRTVGFV, Cities B08F5MD2Y6, Armada B08FRSYGXM, Edifice B0BSR43PY1. I put them in the notes column, not the ASIN column.
- The base-game ASINs come from BGG affiliate links whose titles match: 7 Wonders (Second Edition) B08F65MX4L and Race for the Galaxy B000YLAOEW.
