## Gotchas
- Amazon `/dp` fetches often return an empty or tiny page on the first attempt; the empty ones for the two ASINs above were genuine 404s.
- Publisher release years are reprint dates in places: 2nd Story shows 2018 but was reviewed in Feb 2014 (CSV uses 2014). Tragic Events shows 2019 retail (reviewer noted it earlier as a limited release).
- Tragic Events player count conflicts (publisher 2-5, reviewer 2-6); CSV uses 2-6.
- Extreme Danger is the storage box for earlier expansions; other expansions are cardboard-wrapped packs and are mostly silos that do not mix, except Honor & Duty's attic/basement board, which needs Extreme Danger or 2nd Story.
