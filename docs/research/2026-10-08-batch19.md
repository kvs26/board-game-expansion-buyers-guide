## Gotchas
- Age of Artisans year (2020) comes from Board Game Quest's overview; its BGG page is behind a Cloudflare challenge. Its 6-player max is from the Amazon title and Board Game Quest.
- Tomesaga year (2020) is from BoardGameVideo's snippet; players 2-6 from the Amazon title. It is a trilogy product, listed under Architects but needs Paladins and Viscounts too.
- Works of Wonder does not require Age of Artisans, though BGG lists it as expanding both.
- Amazon title checks: B07BHHC4S1 base, B082N5N7XB, B09SK21DZT, B085T8VNRC (all 200 + matching title). B09TTJW2Y4 is the Collector's Box organizer (not an expansion).
