## Sources
- BGG item data through the geekdo API (`api.geekdo.com/api/geekitems`, `dynamicinfo`): names, years, players, ratings, rating counts, descriptions and the canonical links used in every BGG reference row. BGG web pages are behind a Cloudflare challenge.
- BGG forum threads (geekdo articles API), used for Earth (Abundance and Animal Kingdom threads), Wyrmspan, Ares Expedition and BANG!. Munchkin threads are sparse; for Munchkin: 3595286 (2nd Edition announcement), 3472289 (Unnatural Axe card counts), 313096 (set icons from Munchkin 4 on), 2871116 (expansions are designed to be mixed).
- Publisher and shop pages:
  - Earth: Inside Up Games product pages.
  - Wyrmspan: Stonemaier Games store.
  - Ares Expedition: Stronghold Games pages and Highlander Hobbies (retailer).
  - BANG!: dV Giochi (Italian shop, EUR prices).
  - Munchkin: munchkin.game product pages (SRP, card counts, descriptions) and Warehouse 23 (`warehouse23.com/products/<handle>`, Steve Jackson Games' store; product JSON for the Big Box contents).
- Amazon `/dp/<ASIN>` pages for ASIN-to-title checks.

## Edition caveats
- Munchkin Big Box (June 2025, $134.95, SJG1568) contains the original 168 cards plus Munchkin 2, 3, 6 (Dungeons), Bosses, Side Quests and Side Quests 2, 50 new cards and a gameboard (Warehouse 23 product JSON). Buyers of the Big Box already own those expansions.
- Munchkin Deluxe (July 2025) exists. A BGG thread (3595286, Oct 2025) says a Munchkin 2nd Edition was announced on BackerKit; not confirmed from a publisher page.
- Munchkin Game Changers collects Fairy Dust, Munchkinomicon, Monster Enhancers and Reloaded plus Fairy Dust Dice cards (68 cards, $24.95) and is out of print. Double Dungeons (40 double-size Dungeons and 32 Portals) collects Demented Dungeons (BGG 33493) and Terrible Tombs (BGG 193528); BGG also lists Double Dungeons as 297107. Munchkinomicon has a duplicate BGG item, 312905.
- Munchkin publisher pages show reprint dates, so the BGG year is used (Fairy Dust page April 2010 vs BGG 2009; Hidden Treasures page September 2017 vs BGG 2015). A 2013 Unnatural Axe printing had 108 cards against 112 now. Cards carry set icons from Munchkin 4 on.
- The base Munchkin Warehouse 23 page returns 404, so the base-game link is the munchkin.game page plus a BGG row. Seven out-of-print expansions (Fairy Dust, Munchkinomicon, Monster Enhancers, Reloaded, Game Changers, Hidden Treasures, Bosses) have a publisher page and a BGG row only.
- BANG!: Wild West Show, The Valley of Shadows, High Noon and A Fistful of Cards are sold only inside the "BANG! Expansion Pack" bundle on dV Giochi, so a buyer cannot pick them one at a time. Prices there are in EUR, so `price_usd` is blank.
- Ares Expedition: no USD prices were verified, so `price_usd` is blank for all three expansions.
- Earth: Animal Kingdom (2026) is new; the publisher page does not say whether Abundance is required.
