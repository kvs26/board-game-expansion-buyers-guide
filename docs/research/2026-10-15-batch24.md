## Edition caveats
- Legendary: the ASIN and recommendations point to the Second Edition core set (a forum poster recommends it; it adds Special Officers, Special Bystanders and Sidekicks). BGG descriptions say each expansion needs a core set; full backward compatibility of every older expansion with the Second Edition core was not independently checked. The 2021 blog says the big boxes are out of print; not re-checked for 2026.
- Mage Knight: the WizKids Ultimate Edition ($124.99) bundles the base game, The Lost Legion, Krang and Shades of Tezla. The Lost Legion's shop link is that Ultimate Edition page.
- The Apocalypse Dragon: BGG lists 2026 but the WizKids listing was published 2024-03-07; BGG year used.
- El Dorado: a BGG poster says the Scandinavian (999 / Lautapelit) expansions are not compatible with the Ravensburger base game because of card size. A forum thread says US buyers order from Lautapelit.fi; no US link was verified.
- Eclipse: Second Dawn only; first-edition expansions are out of scope. Minor Species is also in the Worlds Afar Collection.
