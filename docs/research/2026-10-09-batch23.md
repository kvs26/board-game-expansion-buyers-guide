## Edition caveats
- Burgundy modules are bundled in the 2019 20th Anniversary and 2023 Special Edition; check which edition you own before buying.
- HeroQuest: only the 2021+ Hasbro/Avalon Hill line is covered. Original 1989 packs are out of scope.
- War of the Ring: Second Edition only. The first-edition Battles of the Third Age module is not recommended with it.
- Terra Mystica: Fan Factions is an official Capstone product (2025), separate from the free fan print-and-play PDF.
