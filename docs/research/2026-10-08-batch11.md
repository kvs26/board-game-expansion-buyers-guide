Date: 2026-10-08. Output: `raw-source/research-batch11-games.csv`, `raw-source/research-batch11.csv`, `raw-source/buy-links/disney-villainous.csv`.

## Sources and gotchas

- Release years: Wikipedia "Villainous (board game)".
- Prices, players and play time: ravensburger.us product pages (list price used; sale prices such as 29.74 for Introduction to Evil and 19.54 for Sugar and Spite were ignored).
- Despicable Plots and Bigger and Badder have no ravensburger.us product page; their 34.99 price is inferred from sibling 3-villain boxes and is unconfirmed.
- Amazon `/dp/` curl is throttled under load (many NOTITLE results); `fetch_webpage` was used as a fallback. Some ASINs were confirmed from Amazon's own brand carousel rather than a direct title curl (Perfectly Wretched, Bigger and Badder, Filled with Fright, Sugar and Spite).
- Wicked to the Core B07PRNJJW6 showed a cannot-ship banner in the fetch locale; the title matches.
- Despicable Plots has two listings: B091ZGHQ7P (used, title verified) and B08VQD7XVZ (currently unavailable).
